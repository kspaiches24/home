Karen Spaiches, maker and owner

My story

It started with a
quiet afternoon.

My love of crafting began when I was young. I was always happiest creating something with my hands and discovering how simple materials could become a gift with a story of its own.

After building a 20-year career and raising my two boys, I was ready for a new chapter. In 2020, I moved to beautiful Savannah, Georgia, and eventually made the brave decision to leave my longtime career behind and pursue the creative work I had loved for so many years.

Peach & Thread grew from that leap of faith. Today, every card and cross-stitch piece is designed and finished by me in Savannah, with patience, thoughtful details, and the belief that handmade things carry a little more love.
